# PAG: Group Meetings

While we focus on answering questions related to enrollment, we know an
implied mission is assisting in the development of those who want to do
similar work. To this end, we hold group meetings where we discuss

- Relevant research articles
- Application of function-specific applications
- Examples of new (to us) coding methods
- Our research success and setbacks

We invite and welcome those outside of our group to join our meetings.
Please contact
[Emilio](mailto:emilio@msu.edu?subject=I%20want%20to%20attend%20PAG%20group%20meetings!)
for meeting information.

## Group Meeting Schedule

Group Meeting are every couple of weeks where the current and past
members come together to share updates about current and past work. Our
Group Meeting schedule and agenda is loose. Each meeting starts with a
quick “stand-up” where everyone briefly talks about…

- what they are working on
- one thing causing grief
- one victory

***The meetings are informal discussions on current projects and help
overcoming coding and research obstacles.***

Everyone is welcome to attend! Please contact
[Emilio](mailto:emilio@msu.edu?subject=I%20want%20to%20attend%20PAG%20group%20meetings!)
for day, time and location (usually a local coffee shop). Sometimes we
meet on Zoom; email Emilio for the link.

## Book Club

Book club is structured [professional
development](https://emilioxavier.github.io/theHUB/articles/web_only/PAG_ProfessionalDevelopment.md).
The goal is to gain knowledge and insight to current projects through
the perspective of someone else.
